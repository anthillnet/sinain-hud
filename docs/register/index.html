Data terms
  1. Account data. We store your email address and the OAuth identity your provider shares, plus your waitlist status. Nothing else is collected at signup.
  2. Your content stays on your machine. Screen text, audio transcripts, and saved knowledge are processed on your device or through the AI providers you configure — they are not sent to or stored on Sinain servers.
  3. Redaction first. Passwords, credit-card numbers, API keys, and anything wrapped in <private> tags are wiped before anything is remembered.
  4. Pro session access is opt-in. In Pro, the Sinain agent connects to your laptop session only after you approve it on the device. Every connection is visible while active and can be revoked at any time.
  5. No selling, no training. We do not sell your data and do not train models on your content.
  6. Deletion. Email support@sinain.com to delete your account and waitlist data at any time.
  7. The full Privacy Policy governs anything not covered here.

What Pro unlocks

In Pro, the Sinain agent can connect to your laptop session and work alongside you. It sees the screen context you already share with Sinain, picks up tasks in flight, and acts in your session with your permission — like a colleague looking over your shoulder who can also grab the keyboard when you ask.
