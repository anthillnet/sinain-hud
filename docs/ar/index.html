§ 01 — How it works

Three taps and you're talking.

No setup, nothing to learn. Open your camera, point at something, and ask — Sinain answers out loud and highlights what you're asking about.

Step 1

Open your camera

Tap once to start. It opens right in your phone's browser — no app, no sign-up hoops.

Step 2

Point or tap

Aim at whatever you're curious about — or tap it on the screen. Sinain highlights it and keeps it in its sights.

Step 3

Just ask

Talk to it like a person — "what is this?", "how do I fix it?" — and it answers out loud. Cut in any time.

§ 02 — What you can do

If you can point at it, you can ask about it.

A few things people use it for. There's no menu to learn — just aim and talk.

“What is this?”

A plant, a gadget, a landmark, a bug on the wall, a bottle of wine — point and find out in seconds.

“How do I fix it?”

A blinking light, a leaky tap, an error on a screen, a button you can't figure out. Show it and get walked through it.

“What can I make?”

Point at what's in the fridge or on the counter and get ideas for dinner — with what you actually have.

“What does this say?”

A menu, a sign, a label in another language. Aim your phone and Sinain reads and explains it.

“Walk me through it.”

Setting something up, assembling furniture, using a new device — step by step, hands-free, as you go.

“Keep an eye on that.”

Tap or name a thing and the highlight follows it as you move — so you never lose what you're working on.

§ 03 — It comes to meetings too

Send it to your video calls.

Paste a Teams or Google Meet link and Sinain joins like a guest. It follows along and takes notes — so afterward you can just ask "what did we decide?"

  • Joins with a link — let "Sinain (AI)" in and it's there.
  • Takes the notes — so you can stay in the conversation.
  • Remembers for you — ask it anything about the call later.

§ 04 — Privacy

Your camera stays yours.

It only looks when there's something worth looking at, your video is encrypted end-to-end, and sensitive details are scrubbed before anything is saved. Only you can get into your Sinain.

Encrypted
Your video and voice are scrambled end-to-end — no one in between can see them.
Only when needed
It looks when something actually changes — it isn't recording your whole day.
Nothing private saved
Emails, card numbers and passwords are wiped before anything is remembered.
Just you
You sign in to get in — no shared logins, no one else in your Sinain.
