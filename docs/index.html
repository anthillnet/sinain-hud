§ 02 — What Sinain does

It turns your recent work into useful help.

Sinain notices what matters, organizes it into a clear summary, and gives it to the AI you choose. You stay in control of what is remembered or shared.

01 / capture

Understands your screen and meetings.

Sinain can watch your screen and listen to system audio while automatically hiding sensitive things like passwords, API keys, and private blocks before anything is sent out.

macOS nativescreen + audioOCR included
02 / distill

Remembers decisions and loose ends.

Sinain turns messy work activity into clear memory: people, projects, decisions, open questions, and why something was chosen. It remembers useful details so your AI can answer with the right background later — 82.8% IPR on LongMemEval (public benchmark).

03 / access

Gives you help where you work.

Your connected AI tools can use the same live background you see. You can also browse it in Sinain or use the small on-screen helper, which stays hidden from screenshots and screen shares.

§ 02 — Privacy

You decide what Sinain remembers.

Sinain keeps recent activity temporary until you choose to save or share it. It can hide passwords and other sensitive details before using online AI, and you can also run it fully offline.

Nothing saved by default

Recent activity disappears unless you choose to remember it.

Sensitive details hidden

Passwords, keys, and payment details can be removed before online AI sees anything.

Offline when you need it

Keep processing on your Mac when work should not leave your computer.
