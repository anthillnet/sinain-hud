Sinain · feedback prompt · standalone
Asking "is this worth continuing?" — at the right moment
A one-time, dismissable nudge that links out to the experience survey (discussion #231). Not part of attention triage — it's its own surface, fired by a calendar/milestone rule, never by the WSM.
⏱
The firing rule. Fires once, on the first value-proof moment — whichever comes first: a thread resolved, a context enrichment, or a chat interaction. That's the instant the user has just felt the value, so the question lands while the impression is fresh. Shown at that breakpoint; never during onboarding, never mid-task.
