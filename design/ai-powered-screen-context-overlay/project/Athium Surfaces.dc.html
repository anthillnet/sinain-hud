6 · P1 — the attention cockpit (overlay UI)
The board becomes a Flutter surface in the overlay — an NSPanel sharingType .none, invisible to screen capture, so it's a private cockpit for the user's own glance. That forces it to be ambient and glanceable — not a dashboard.
!
Honesty rendering (binding). Unlike the /workstate inspector above (an eng/debug view, decimals fine), the cockpit shows qualitative tiers only — never raw scores. Need is order + a 3-tier glyph (⚠ now · ◷ soon · · later), warmth is color-cooled. Checkable evidence ("left with errors · 1d"), never affect ("you seem frustrated"). stuckness may color a card but carries no number and no "you're stuck" text.
